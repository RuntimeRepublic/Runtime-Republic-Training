# 1. Purpose

Defines which teams Runtime Republic trains, how a team's party affiliation is handled,
and where paid software and consulting belong.

---

# 2. Core Principle

**Runtime Republic training is nonpartisan.** Any team that supports the AGCL control
documents as directional goals may be trained, whatever party it declares, or none.

---

# 3. Rules

## 3.1 Who may be trained

1. Runtime Republic trains any team that supports the canonical AGCL control documents
   (CONTROL 01 §6) as directional goals.
2. A person is a member only if they support at least one of the AGCL Points
   ([Points 01–10](https://github.com/jhodges07/AGCL-Control-Documents#core-points)).
   Supporting one Point is enough; a member may disagree with the rest.
3. A team supports the AGCL as directional goals when every one of its members meets
   rule 2. No repository or fee is required.
4. The Human Engineer accepts each team. Accepted teams are recorded in
   {{FILL: where accepted teams and their written statements are kept}}.
5. A person who no longer supports any AGCL Point is no longer a member. A team that
   withdraws its support receives no further team training.

## 3.2 Party affiliation

6. A team may declare a party affiliation, or none. Declaring is optional.
7. A declared party never decides whether a team is trained or what it is taught.
8. Every team receives the same course material. There are no party-specific versions.
9. Lessons, examples and podcasts never favor or oppose a party or candidate, and never
   name a trained team's party.

## 3.3 Conscience

10. No member is asked to profess faith. Scripture is the chief control of the system's
    design (CONTROL 01 §6; CONTROL 09), not a test of membership. Supporting one AGCL Point
    stays the only bar (rule 2).

## 3.4 Public lessons stay open

11. Published lessons remain open to anyone under CC BY 4.0, whether or not they are on a
   team. This document governs organized team training only (sessions, cohorts, support).

## 3.5 Software and consulting

12. Paid software and consulting are offered by Lean Office Pro LLC, not Runtime Republic.
13. Training eligibility never depends on buying anything from Lean Office Pro LLC.

---

# 4. Edge Cases

| Case | Handling |
|---|---|
| Team declares a party | Trained the same as any other team (rules 7–8) |
| Team declares no party | Trained the same as any other team |
| Person supports one AGCL Point and opposes the rest | Member (rule 2) |
| Person supports no AGCL Point | Not a member; public lessons stay open (rules 2, 11) |
| One person on a team supports no AGCL Point | That person is not a member; the team is eligible once every member meets rule 2 (rule 3) |
| Person does not share the Bible's authority or any faith | Member if they support one Point; never asked to profess faith (rule 10) |
| Team asks for a version slanted to its party | Declined; same material for all (rule 8) |
| Team wants only paid software or consulting | Referred to Lean Office Pro LLC (rule 12) |
| Team withdraws its support | No further team training; public lessons stay open (rules 5, 11) |
| Person not on any team | May use every published lesson (rule 11) |

# 5. Enforcement

• Membership and team acceptance (rules 1–5) is a Human Engineer decision, checked by review.
• Lesson neutrality (rule 9) is checked in lesson review before a lesson is Approved
  (CONTROL 05 §4).
• No script enforces this document.
