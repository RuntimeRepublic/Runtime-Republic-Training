# 1. Purpose

Defines which teams Runtime Republic trains, how a team's party affiliation is handled,
and where paid software and consulting belong.

---

# 2. Core Principle

**Runtime Republic training is nonpartisan.** Any team that supports the AGCL control
documents as directional goals may be trained, whatever party it declares, or none.

---

# 3. Rules

## 3.1 Who may be trained

1. Runtime Republic trains any team that supports the canonical AGCL control documents
   (CONTROL 01 §6) as directional goals.
2. "Supports as directional goals" means the team states in writing that it works toward
   the AGCL's goals. It does not require adopting every rule, a repository, or a fee.
3. The Human Engineer accepts each team. Accepted teams are recorded in
   {{FILL: where accepted teams and their written statements are kept}}.
4. A team that withdraws its support receives no further team training.

## 3.2 Party affiliation

5. A team may declare a party affiliation, or none. Declaring is optional.
6. A declared party never decides whether a team is trained or what it is taught.
7. Every team receives the same course material. There are no party-specific versions.
8. Lessons, examples and podcasts never favor or oppose a party or candidate, and never
   name a trained team's party.

## 3.3 Public lessons stay open

9. Published lessons remain open to anyone under CC BY 4.0, whether or not they are on a
   team. This document governs organized team training only (sessions, cohorts, support).

## 3.4 Software and consulting

10. Paid software and consulting are offered by Lean Office Pro LLC, not Runtime Republic.
11. Training eligibility never depends on buying anything from Lean Office Pro LLC.

---

# 4. Edge Cases

| Case | Handling |
|---|---|
| Team declares a party | Trained the same as any other team (rules 6–7) |
| Team declares no party | Trained the same as any other team |
| Team supports the AGCL's goals but disagrees with some rules | Eligible; directional support is enough (rule 2) |
| Team asks for a version slanted to its party | Declined; same material for all (rule 7) |
| Team wants only paid software or consulting | Referred to Lean Office Pro LLC (rule 10) |
| Team withdraws its support | No further team training; public lessons stay open (rules 4, 9) |
| Person not on any team | May use every published lesson (rule 9) |

---

# 5. Enforcement

• Team acceptance (rules 1–4) is a Human Engineer decision, checked by review.
• Lesson neutrality (rule 8) is checked in lesson review before a lesson is Approved
  (CONTROL 05 §4).
• No script enforces this document.
