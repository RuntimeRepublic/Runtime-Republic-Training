A civic team's first real fight wasn't about taxes. It was about a beer keg.

**Cut. Beep!**

It started with a cookout. Forty-two dollars for burgers. Linda went to approve it and stopped. Is this something our money is for? Nobody had ever said.

**Cut. Beep!**

So they wrote the powers down, the way Madison promised for government. Not on the list? The money can't buy it. Running the group: yes. Food at team events: yes.

**Cut. Beep!**

Then the survey's next line. A beer keg. The room split right down the middle.

**Cut. Beep!**

Then Dana saw the catch. Every gift goes into one pool, and spending draws from it at random. Walt could look up his receipt code and find his ten dollars bought beer. With no say in it.

**Cut. Beep!**

That's when they realized they hadn't built a bookkeeping system. They'd built a small republic. Next time: three ways to settle the keg. Only one works. Subscribe. More at RuntimeRepublic.com.
