# Production notes: Short 01, Every Dollar in the Open

Not narrated. Images, editing and YouTube text for this Short.

## Editing

- Record the read-aloud of `lesson.md` with Camtasia (system audio only).
- Each **Cut. Beep!** marks a cut. Delete everything before the first beep, each beep, and everything after the last.
- Image 1 runs from the start to the second beep, image 2 to the third, and so on.
- Start from the Camtasia project `Episode 01 Short v2` (vertical canvas, locked black bar and website).

## ChatGPT setup (paste once at the start of a new chat)

```
I'm making a series of vertical images for a YouTube Short. I'll send one scene at a time. Make one image per message and wait for my next scene.

RULES FOR EVERY IMAGE
- Vertical 9:16, 1080 x 1920 PNG.
- Warm, realistic editorial illustration style, like a painted magazine feature: soft natural light, Kansas setting. The same style in every image.
- NO website names, NO titles, NO footer, NO branding text of any kind. Only text that is part of the scene, like a sign or a whiteboard, spelled exactly as I write it.
- BOTTOM 20%: plain floor, table edge or prairie ground only. A black bar will cover it in editing. No faces, hands, objects or text there.
- Keep faces and text away from the right edge, where the app's buttons sit.
- No real people, no logos or brand names, no party symbols. Nonpartisan.

CAST (keep each the same in every image)
- Dana: woman in her 30s, dark hair in a ponytail, denim jacket.
- Mike: man in his 40s, ball cap, brown work jacket. The treasurer.
- Linda: woman in her 60s, short silver hair, glasses, navy cardigan. Usually at a laptop.
- Walt: older man, gray beard, glasses, plaid flannel shirt.
- Dale: easygoing man in his 40s, palm-print shirt, cargo shorts, always wearing a yellow hard hat with two plain unlabeled beer cans strapped to the sides and siphon straws to his mouth. Calm, never sloppy.
- Harvest (Shorts 04-06 only): retired machinist, 67, gray hair, weathered hands, tan work shirt.
Setting: a small-town meeting room with a folding table and a window onto the prairie, unless the scene says otherwise.
```
