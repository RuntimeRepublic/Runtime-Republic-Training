Harvest is sixty-seven. Forty-six years of Social Security came out of his paycheck. So he asked: what's the difference?

**Cut. Beep!**

Walt drew two columns. Who owns it? Today, not you. The Supreme Court said so in 1960. Paying the tax gives you an expectation, not property. Under AGCL Point 05.1, the fund is yours.

**Cut. Beep!**

Can your family inherit it? Harvest said quietly, my brother paid in forty years and died at sixty-four. His kids got nothing from it.

**Cut. Beep!**

Who borrows the money? Today, government spent the surplus and left IOUs. Under 05.1, the Treasury borrows from you first, and the interest is yours.

**Cut. Beep!**

And the clock is real. The trustees' own 2026 report says the retirement trust fund runs dry in 2032. After that, taxes cover about seventy-eight percent of benefits. Harvest did the math. I'll be seventy-three.
