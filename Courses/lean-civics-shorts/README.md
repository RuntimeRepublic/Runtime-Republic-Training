# Lean Civics Shorts

**Status:** Draft. One script so far. Nothing here is approved.

Vertical (9:16) cartoon Shorts for YouTube Shorts and Facebook Reels, each under three
minutes. Every script opens with about 15 seconds on the Lean Civics Challenge, then tells one
story from the Every Dollar in the Open series as a short story read by one narrator, and
ends on an end card that asks viewers to subscribe and visit RuntimeRepublic.com.

**How a script is recorded:** the narration is recorded reading the script aloud and edited in
Camtasia. Each bold **Image** line is a swap marker: it is read aloud with a pause on each side
so it is easy to find in the waveform, then cut, and the matching image is placed at that spot.
Keep faces and text out of the bottom third and the right edge of each image, where the
platforms place buttons and captions.

**The team (first names on screen; call signs online):** Dana (Ridgeline), Mike, the treasurer
(Ledger), Linda, the admin (Gavel), Walt (Longbow), Dale (Tailgate). First names and the
Ledger and Gavel call signs are new in this series (Human Engineer, 2026-11-18).

**Open item:** the Lean Civics Challenge goal of paying off the national debt by 2076 is stated
by the Human Engineer and is not yet written in a source document.
