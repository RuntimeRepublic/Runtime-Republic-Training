But Hamilton laid a stone in 1790: a sinking fund to pay the debt down. AGCL Point 05.1 carries it forward. Government gets one chance to spend it right. Spending the Constitution doesn't list can't use debt, and it expires unless renewed. What government doesn't spend right goes into your own fund, and the Treasury borrows from you first.

Rule of 72. At six percent, money doubles every twelve years. A hundred dollars at thirty is about eight hundred by sixty-six.
