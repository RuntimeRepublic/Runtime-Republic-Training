Forty-six years of Social Security, and Harvest never owned a dime of it. This week, the team tried to change that.

**Cut. Beep!**

AGCL Point 04 says every month you paid counts on its own, your half and your employer's. Each month's dollars become gold weight at that month's price. Then it's locked.

**Cut. Beep!**

Why gold? The Framers chose it. Article One, Section Ten names gold and silver coin. A 1978 dollar shrank. Gold weight didn't.

**Cut. Beep!**

Then Harvest opened his folder. One number per year. No months. The rule needs monthly data, so Linda logged it as a finding, not a complaint.

**Cut. Beep!**

The rest: a personal gold ledger, inheritable, first in line to be repaid, and nobody borrows it without your say. Not a new benefit, Walt said. A receipt for what you already paid. Harvest said, you track ten-dollar blocks. Track mine.
