**Cut. Beep!**

Walt said pass a hat at the party, cash, off the books. Dale shot it down. Cash off the books is how the old mess comes back.

**Cut. Beep!**

Dana said let donors flag their money, no alcohol. Mike's AI assistant ran the numbers. Flagging just pushed the keg onto somebody else's money.

**Cut. Beep!**

Then Mike's idea: two pools. The general pool pays for what everyone agreed to. The function pool takes only money people choose to give. No money? No keg. That's an answer too.

**Cut. Beep!**

Walt said Jefferson settled this long ago. Your money follows your consent. Dale grinned. So beer is a consumption tax. Walt didn't blink. Beer has always been a consumption tax.
