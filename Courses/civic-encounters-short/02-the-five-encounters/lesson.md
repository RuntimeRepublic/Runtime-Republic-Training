The Civic Encounters Challenge has five levels, named after the movie's famous scale. Except here, the encounters are with government.

Encounter one is the Sighting. You know something is taking your money, but you don't track it. Most people live here. There's no shame in it. It's just where everyone starts. The goal isn't to feel bad about where you are. The goal is to move up one level at a time.

Encounter two is Physical Evidence. You've run a real budget for a full month and closed it. Every dollar has a place. Every charge has a receipt. No more guessing.

Encounter three is Contact. You've identified every government that takes your money, including the taxes and fees hiding on your bills. You stop wondering who's out there. You know their names.

Encounter four is Communication. You've contacted the officials who actually set your taxes, and you kept a record. Not Congress for your property tax. The county, the city, the school board. Now you're the one sending signals.

Encounter five is First Contact Initiated. Your records meet the standards of the Runtime Republic frameworks. You're not waiting for them to land on you. You're setting the terms.

Why build it this way? Because a tax you can't see is a tax you can't judge. Adam Smith wrote in 1776, "The tax which each individual is bound to pay ought to be certain, and not arbitrary." Certain means you know what you pay, when, and to whom. Each encounter makes your taxes a little more certain.

And here's the most important rule. Every level is earned with evidence and checked by a reviewer. First an AI checks it. Then a person on another team checks it. No claiming a level. You have to show it.

That's what makes a level worth talking about. When someone says they're a CE3, you know it means something. They didn't just say it. They proved it. And they can help you do the same.

## Recap

Let's sum up. The five encounters are Sighting, Physical Evidence, Contact, Communication and First Contact Initiated. Adam Smith said a tax should be certain, not arbitrary, and each level makes yours more certain. Every level is earned with evidence and checked by a reviewer. Want the full story?
