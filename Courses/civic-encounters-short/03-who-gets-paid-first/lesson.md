# Who Gets Paid First?

Here's a question that sounds simple. Who gets paid first in your house?

Ask most people, and they'll say family. Food, shelter, keeping the lights on, getting to work, saving for tomorrow. That's the order on paper. It's the one we'd choose.

Now look at how the money actually moves.

Withholding is taken before your paycheck ever reaches you. You don't get a vote on the timing. It's simply gone. Most people never even see the full amount they earned.

Property tax has to be paid, or the house is at risk. Miss it long enough and you can lose the home your family lives in.

Bill taxes and fees are added before you can pay for the service itself. You can't buy the electricity without paying the taxes on it. The same goes for your phone, your water and often your trash pickup.

So in practice, government often gets paid first. The family gets what's left.

That's the real budget order. Not the order you'd like. Not the order a candidate promised. The order your own records show.

Once you can see it, you can make real choices. Thomas Sowell said it plainly: "There are no solutions. There are only trade-offs." You can't wish taxes away. But you can see exactly what each one costs your family, and decide which trade-offs you're willing to accept.

And you can take that evidence to the people who set those taxes. Not with a complaint. With a count, a ranking and a question.

Try it this week. Write down your top five spending categories by what you actually pay. Then mark where government lands. For a lot of families, it's number one or number two.

That's not a reason to be angry. It's a reason to pay attention. Because what you measure, you can change. And once you've measured it, you can show your neighbors how to do the same.

## Recap

Let's sum up. Most families say family comes first. Their records usually say government gets paid first, through withholding, property tax and bill fees. Thomas Sowell reminds us there are only trade-offs. Rank your real budget order and see where government lands. Want the full story?
