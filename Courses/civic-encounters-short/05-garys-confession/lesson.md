My buddy Gary pulled me aside and whispered, "Promise you won't tell anyone. I think I've been probed. By aliens. It happens every time I look at my budget."

Every time Gary opens his bank app, he gets a cold, empty feeling. Money is missing. He loses whole weekends trying to figure it out. And he sees bright lights.

The bright lights are his phone at two in the morning. The rest is real.

So we sat down with three pieces of evidence.

His pay stub was covered in strange markings. Federal income tax. Social Security and Medicare. State income tax. All taken before Gary saw a dime. They get him every payday, before he even lands.

His phone bill had small lines at the bottom: taxes, fees and surcharges. Some go to governments. Some are just the phone company calling its own charges fees. You have to check which is which.

Then his property tax notice. Gary admitted he never opens that one. "It glows," he said.

We opened it anyway and counted the names. State. County. City. School district. And three special districts he'd never heard of. Seven governments, all landing on one house.

"Every one of them promised me something," Gary said. "Nobody ever told me what it would cost."

Milton Friedman had a short answer for that: "There's no such thing as a free lunch." Every promise gets paid for. The only question is whether you can see the bill.

"So I'm not crazy?" Gary asked.

No, Gary. You're a CE1. Civic Encounter, Level One: the Sighting. You know something is taking your money, but you've never tracked it.

Gary stood up. "One month. CE2. Then I'm going for Contact."

Then he asked, "What's a CE zero?"

That's the guy who never opens the envelope. Gary's not going back there. Are you?

## Recap

Let's sum up. Gary thought aliens were taking his money. It was government: taxes on his paycheck, fees on his bills, and seven governments on his property tax notice. Milton Friedman reminds us there's no such thing as a free lunch. Gary is a CE1, and he's going up. Want the full story?
