This one is a skit for two voices. Gary is worried. Mike has been tracking his money.

Gary: Mike. Can I tell you something? You have to promise not to tell anyone.

Mike: Sure, Gary.

Gary: I think I've been probed.

Mike: Probed.

Gary: By aliens. It happens every time I look at my budget. I sit down, open my bank app, and I feel this cold, empty feeling. Money's missing. I don't know where it went. I lose time. Sometimes a whole weekend.

Mike: Gary, it's not aliens.

Gary: Then explain the bright lights.

Mike: That's your phone screen at two in the morning.

Gary: Okay, explain the missing money.

Mike: Let's look. Got your pay stub?

Gary: See? Look at all these strange markings. It's a language I don't understand.

Mike: That's federal income tax, Social Security and Medicare, and state income tax. All taken before you ever saw a dime.

Gary: So they get me before I even land.

Mike: Every payday. Now, your phone bill.

Gary: Seventy bucks a month.

Mike: Look at the bottom. See those little lines? Taxes, fees, surcharges. Some go to governments. Some are the phone company calling its own charges fees. You have to check which is which.

Gary: They're hiding in the small print?

Mike: Where else would they hide? And your property tax notice?

Gary: I don't open that one. It glows.

Mike: Open it. Count the names.

Gary: State. County. City. School district. And three districts I've never heard of. Seven, Mike. There are seven of them.

Mike: And they've been here the whole time.

Gary: So I'm not crazy.

Mike: You're not crazy. You're a CE1.

Gary: A what?

Mike: Civic Encounter, Level One. The Sighting. You know something is taking your money, but you've never tracked it. Most people are CE1.

Gary: How do I get out of here?

Mike: You go up. Next is CE2, Physical Evidence. You run a real budget for one full month and close it out. Every dollar has a place, and every charge has a receipt.

Gary: Evidence. I like evidence. Then what?

Mike: Then CE3, the big one. Contact. That's where I want you. You identify every government that takes your money, including the ones hiding on your bills. You stop guessing who's out there. You know their names.

Gary: I make contact.

Mike: You make contact. And here's the payoff. You can finally rank who gets paid first in your house. Family, or government. Most people say family. Their records usually say government.

Gary: That's the cold feeling.

Mike: That's the cold feeling.

Gary: Is there anything past three?

Mike: Two more. CE4 is Communication. You contact the officials who actually set your taxes, and you keep a record. Not Congress for your property tax, by the way. The county, the city, the school board.

Gary: So I'm the one sending signals now.

Mike: Exactly. And CE5 is First Contact Initiated. Your records meet the standards of the Runtime Republic frameworks. You're not waiting for them to land on you. You're setting the terms.

Gary: And I just declare that I'm a CE5?

Mike: Nope. Every level is earned with evidence, and a reviewer checks it. No claiming. You have to show it.

Gary: Mike, I'm going to do it. One month. CE2. Then I'm going for Contact.

Mike: Good. Start with three documents. A pay stub, one bill, one tax notice. Count the governments.

Gary: And I can tell people about this?

Mike: Tell everybody. Just don't post dollar amounts or your address. Share your level and your count.

Gary: I'm a CE1, working on CE2. Seven governments landed on my house.

Mike: That's the Civic Encounters Challenge.

Gary: Mike. What's a CE zero?

Mike: That's the guy who never opens the envelope.

Gary: I'm not going back there.

## Recap

Let's sum up. Gary thought aliens were taking his money. Mike showed him it was government: taxes on his paycheck, fees on his phone bill, and seven governments on his property tax notice. Gary starts at CE1, the Sighting, and aims for CE2 and then CE3, Contact, where he can finally rank who gets paid first in his house. Every level is earned with evidence. Find out who's landing on your house. Take the Civic Encounters Challenge. Thanks for listening. This has been Runtime Republic.
