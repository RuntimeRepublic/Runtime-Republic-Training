# It's Not Aliens: A Confession About My Budget

My buddy Gary pulled me aside last week. He looked over both shoulders and whispered, "Promise you won't tell anyone. I think I've been probed."

"Probed?" I said.

"By aliens. It happens every time I look at my budget."

He wasn't joking. Every time Gary opens his bank app, he gets a cold, empty feeling. Money is missing. He can't say where it went. He loses whole weekends trying to figure it out. And he sees bright lights.

The bright lights are his phone at two in the morning. The rest is real. Something is taking Gary's money. It just isn't coming from outer space.

So we sat down with three pieces of evidence: a pay stub, a phone bill and a property tax notice.

The pay stub was covered in strange markings. Federal income tax. Social Security and Medicare. State income tax. All of it was taken before Gary ever saw a dime. They get him every payday, before he even lands.

The phone bill had small lines at the bottom: taxes, fees and surcharges. Some go to governments. Some are just the phone company calling its own charges "fees." You have to check which is which. They hide in the small print, because where else would they hide?

Then came the property tax notice. Gary admitted he never opens that one. "It glows," he said.

We opened it anyway and counted the names. State. County. City. School district. And three special districts Gary had never heard of. Seven governments, all landing on one house.

They've been here the whole time.

"So I'm not crazy?" Gary asked.

No, Gary. You're a CE1.

That's Civic Encounter, Level One: the Sighting. You know something is taking your money, but you've never tracked it. Most people live here. There's no shame in it. But there's a way out, and it goes up.

Level Two is Physical Evidence. You run a real budget for one full month and close it out. Every dollar gets a place. Every charge gets a receipt. No more guessing.

Level Three is the big one: Contact. You identify every government that takes your money, including the ones hiding on your bills. You stop wondering who's out there. You know their names.

Here's the payoff of Contact. You can finally rank who gets paid first in your house: family or government. Most people say family. Their records usually say government. That's the cold feeling Gary kept having.

Level Four is Communication. You contact the officials who actually set your taxes, and you keep a record. Not Congress for your property tax. The county, the city, the school board. Now you're the one sending signals.

Level Five is First Contact Initiated. Your records meet the standards of the Runtime Republic frameworks. You're not waiting for them to land on you. You're setting the terms.

And nobody just declares a level. Every level is earned with evidence and checked by a reviewer. You have to show it.

Gary stood up and said, "One month. CE2. Then I'm going for Contact." I believe him.

Then he asked one more question. "What's a CE zero?"

That's the guy who never opens the envelope.

Gary's not going back there. Are you?

## Recap

Let's sum up. Gary thought aliens were taking his money. It was government: seven of them on one house, plus taxes on his paycheck and fees on his bills. The five Civic Encounter levels run from Sighting to First Contact Initiated, and every level is earned with evidence. Start with three documents, count the governments, and share your level, never your dollar amounts. Thanks for listening. This has been Runtime Republic.
