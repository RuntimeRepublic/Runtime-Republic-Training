# Episode 6 — How the Rules Fit Together: beat sheet (ROUGH DRAFT)

Series: Civic Encounters: Teams (`Courses/civic-encounters-teams`). Status: ROUGH DRAFT for James's approval.
Written by Claude (Opus 5.5), Nov 15, 2026, from the series README (outline, characters, rules, datasets).
Teaching purpose (James): show how AGCL (the U.S. NBLF) works together with NBBF. An NBEF group is described but does not meet.

## Beats
1. **The question.** Walt: "Fine, end property tax. Then what pays for the roads and schools?" Fair question. Mike pulls up the frameworks.
2. **AGCL, the what.** The American Gold Contract for Liberty sets limits: authority must be defined, limited, transparent, measurable and reversible (AGCL Master Framework).
3. **Point 01 and Point 02.** H.R. 25 replaces income and payroll taxes with a visible national sales tax; Point 02 splits that pool 33⅓% federal, 33⅓% state, 33⅓% local, if a state ends income and property taxes (plan/02_revenue_sharing.md).
4. **NBBF, the how.** The Node-Based Budgeting Framework: every budget item is a node with a Human Owner, a cost, outputs, a pass/fail test and a way to end it (NBBF CONTROL 01 §5).
5. **Fitting together.** AGCL says what government may do; NBBF says how every dollar of it is budgeted and tested. Peggy: "It's like double-entry books for government."
6. **The NBEF group, described.** Another team works on the Node-Based Educational Framework for schools. They aren't in this meeting; Sam says the school levy questions belong with them.
7. **One economist, by name, where AGCL supports the point**: a law should be judged by its results, not its intentions.
8. **The smoke test.** Every rule needs a test that shows whether it works, like software (Lean Civics PDCA).
9. **Close.** Gary: "So we're not just against a tax. We're for a system we can check." Seven members now. Someone asks: "Is this getting too big?"

## Recap
- AGCL sets the limits; NBBF budgets and tests every dollar.
- Point 02 is how property tax ends: revenue sharing for states that qualify.
- Every rule needs a smoke test.
