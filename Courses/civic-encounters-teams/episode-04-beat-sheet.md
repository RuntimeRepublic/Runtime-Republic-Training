## Beats
1. **Peggy's exercise.** Everyone lists where their money goes, in the order it leaves: not the order they'd like, the order it actually happens.
2. **Gary's list.** Withholding comes first, before payday. Then rent or mortgage (with property tax inside). Then bills with taxes added on top. Food and family come after.
3. **Linda's list.** Same pattern, with property tax hidden in rent.
4. **Walt's list.** Property tax must be paid or the house can be lost to a tax lien. It comes ahead of almost everything (AGCL 00C Rule C8 on owned property without title).
5. **The four budgets.** Mike shows his AI dashboard: Federal, State, Local, Family. In practice government is paid first, and the family gets what's left.
6. **The question.** "What is your real budget order?" Then: "Does it match your values?" (from the challenge essay).
7. **One economist, by name, where AGCL supports the point**: taxes collected before the citizen sees the money are easy to raise and hard to feel.
8. **What AGCL proposes.** Under Point 01 (H.R. 25, no income or payroll tax) and Point 02 (no property tax), government is paid when the family chooses to buy, so Family moves to first.
9. **Close.** Gary: "So the goal isn't no government. It's family first." Mike: "With evidence." The team agrees to keep tracking. Linda mentions someone Peggy knows at the county.

## Recap
- Real budget order is who gets paid first, not what we'd choose.
- Withholding and tax liens put government ahead of the family today.
- The goal: Family first, proven with evidence.
