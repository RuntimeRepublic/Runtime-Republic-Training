## Beats

1. **Lead-in (no economist quote here, per series rule).** Kitchen table, in person (rule 5).
   Gary stares at his bank balance. Payday was days ago and the money is already thin.
   "I swear aliens are taking it."
2. **Mike agrees it feels that way.** He doesn't laugh. He felt the same a year ago.
3. **Mike's story: the power of AI (James, Nov 14).** Mike runs his budget with AI. The first
   month, it found services and subscriptions he had lost track of, and he cancelled them.
   "I wasn't being robbed by aliens. I'd just lost track."
4. **The turn.** Mike: taxes are the same. Most of us lost track of them a long time ago:
   taken before the paycheck lands, added on top of bills, folded into rent and prices.
   That's CE1: you saw something take your money, but you can't explain it.
5. **The challenge.** Track your money for one month and find every tax. Not just the
   income tax: every line on every bill, receipt and pay stub.
6. **Gary's objection.** He has no laptop. His bills come by mail (rule 7). Mike: start with
   what you've got. Keep the envelopes. We'll meet online next week; join by phone.
7. **One economist, by name, where AGCL supports the point** (series checklist E3): the idea
   that a tax you can't see is a tax you can't judge. Quote chosen from QUOTE-LOG.md, by
   name only, and only if the AGCL documents support it (CONTROL 09 rule 14).
8. **Close.** They shake on it: next week, same time, Gary brings his first three documents
   (a pay stub, a utility bill, a receipt). Mike: "Bring stamps too." Gary: "Why?" Mike:
   "You'll see." (Plants rule 8; paid off in episode 8, Talking Back.)

## Recap (CONTROL 05 style, for the lesson end)
- Most people lost track of their taxes the way they lose track of subscriptions.
- The challenge: one month, find every tax.
- CE1 is where everyone starts: you saw it, but you can't explain it yet.
