## Beats
1. **First online meeting** (rule 5). Mike on his laptop; Gary joins by phone from his kitchen (rule 7), envelopes spread out.
2. **Gary's first finds.** His pay stub alone shows federal income tax, Social Security, Medicare and state tax, all taken before he sees a dollar. His utility bill has lines he never read: a franchise fee and a sales tax. He counts four governments so far.
3. **Mike's AI side.** Mike's AI pulled the same lines from his PDFs in minutes. Gary copies his by hand. The contrast is the point (series goal).
4. **Gary brings a guest.** Linda, his neighbor, rents and has family at home. She heard Gary talking and wants in. She already knows property tax is an issue: her rent went up again, and the landlord blamed taxes.
5. **The renter's blind spot.** Linda never gets a property tax bill, but she pays it inside her rent. How much? No one knows.
6. **The assignment.** Ask your landlord for an estimate of how much of your rent is property tax, and tell him simply: "We're working on ideas to end property taxes." (James)
7. **One economist, by name, where AGCL supports the point** (QUOTE-LOG.md): hidden taxes are harder to judge than visible ones.
8. **Close.** Linda isn't sure she'll get an answer. Mike: "Even no answer is evidence." Gary adds Linda to the circle's chat. Three members now.

## Recap
- Withholding means government is paid before the family sees the money.
- Renters pay property tax too, hidden in rent.
