## Beats

### Part A — The time-lapse (lead-in; no economist quote, per series rule E3)

1. **Saturday morning, Mike's kitchen table.** Coffee, a shoebox of receipts, phone propped
   against the sugar bowl, laptop open to his AI budget. The screen door is open to the porch.
   He taps play. Reagan's voice fills the kitchen. The narration speeds up: time-lapse.
2. **Reagan opens: he's going to talk about controversial things.** Mike logs his **pay stub**.
   Gross pay at the top, take-home at the bottom, and between them the lines most people skip:
   federal withholding, Kansas withholding. The government was paid before Mike was.
3. **"No left or right, only an up or down."** Mike logs his **grocery receipt**. The AI flags
   the sales tax: Kansas dropped its state sales tax on groceries, but local sales tax is
   still on the line. One government moved; others didn't. (Verify both facts in SOURCES.md.)
4. **Urban renewal and property rights.** Mike logs his **mortgage escrow statement**. Part of
   every payment goes to property tax, split among the county, the city, the school district
   and others he has to look up. He notes: "Who are all of these?"
5. **Social Security: "not insurance but … a welfare program"** (Reagan's claim; label it as
   his). Mike goes back to the pay stub and logs the **FICA lines**: Social Security 6.2% and
   Medicare 1.45%, taken before payday.
6. **Taxes: Reagan's "thirty-seven cents of every dollar earned"** (his 1964 figure, not
   today's). Mike logs his **electric bill**. The AI splits it: energy, a franchise fee, sales
   tax, and a provider surcharge marked "not government unless proven." (Series rule: never
   call a provider surcharge a tax without evidence.)
7. **"The nearest thing to eternal life."** Mike logs his **phone bill**: a 911 fee, sales tax,
   and a federal Universal Service charge the carrier passes through. He smiles at the timing.
   (UNVERIFIED story to check before use: the federal telephone excise tax started in 1898 to
   pay for the Spanish-American War and was collected on long distance until 2006.)
8. **Foreign aid and the debt.** Mike logs a **gas receipt**: 18.4 cents a gallon federal,
   24 cents a gallon Kansas (K.S.A. 79-34,141), plus a small state fee, all hidden inside the
   pump price. Nobody sees them on the receipt. (Plants episode 8, the gas tax letter.)
9. **The close of the speech: "a rendezvous with destiny."** The time-lapse slows to normal
   speed. Mike's AI shows the week's tax lines in one column. He circles the total.

### Part B — Gary at the screen door

10. **A knock on the frame.** Gary is on the porch, back from a long week. Through the screen
    he hears the voice before he sees Mike. "Is that Reagan? My dad played that on vinyl."
11. **Mike pauses Spotify** on his iPhone and waves him in. "Coffee's on. Sit down."
12. **Transition: both at the table.** Gary sees the shoebox, the circled number, and the
    column of tax lines. Payday was three days ago and his account is already thin. "I don't
    even know where mine goes." Mike turns the laptop toward him.

### Part C — The talk about taxes

13. **Mike agrees it feels that way.** He doesn't laugh. A year ago he felt the same.
14. **Mike's story: the power of AI (James, Nov 14).** The first month he ran his budget with
    AI, it found subscriptions and services he had lost track of. He cancelled them.
15. **The turn.** Taxes are the same. Most of us lost track of them a long time ago: taken
    before the paycheck lands, added on top of bills, folded into rent and prices. Mike points
    at the column: "Every one of these was in the speech this morning."
16. **The CE1-to-CE2 scale (the series' only alien reference).** Mike: "The challenge borrows
    its levels from UFO sightings. CE1 is the Sighting: you know something is taking your money,
    but you can't prove it. CE2 is Physical Evidence: one month, every receipt, every tax."
    Gary: "So I'm a Sighting." Mike: "So was I." No more aliens after this beat, in any episode.
17. **Up, not left or right.** Gary asks if this is a Republican thing, because of the speech.
    Mike: "Reagan said it himself: not left or right, up or down. The levels go up. Anybody can
    climb them." (Ties to the series purpose: no opposing sides, everyone works it out together.)
18. **The challenge.** Track your money for one month and find every tax. Not just income tax:
    every line on every bill, receipt and pay stub.
19. **Gary's objection.** No laptop. His bills come by mail (rule 7). Mike: "Start with what
    you've got. Keep the envelopes. We'll meet online next week; you can join by phone."
20. **One economist, by name, where AGCL supports the point** (series checklist E3): a tax you
    can't see is a tax you can't judge. Quote chosen from QUOTE-LOG.md, by name only, and only
    if the AGCL documents support it (CONTROL 09 rule 14).
21. **Close.** Gary: "So what's my time for choosing?" Mike: "Tonight. One month." They shake on
    it: next week, same time, Gary brings his first three documents (a pay stub, a utility bill,
    a receipt). Mike: "Bring stamps too." Gary: "Why?" Mike: "You'll see." (Plants rule 8; paid
    off in episode 8, Talking Back.)

## Recap (CONTROL 05 style, for the lesson end)
- Most people lost track of their taxes the way they lose track of subscriptions.
- One ordinary week of receipts holds taxes from several governments, most of them hidden.
- CE1 is the Sighting; CE2 is Physical Evidence. The challenge: one month, find every tax.
