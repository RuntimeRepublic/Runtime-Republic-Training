## Why these sources

All quotes below come from Reagan's speeches as President (1981–1989). Works of the U.S.
government have no copyright (17 U.S.C. § 105), so these quotes can run long and the narrator
can read them freely. The 1964 "A Time for Choosing" speech was given as a private citizen and
its rights are unclear, so it is only introduced, not quoted at length.

Every quote was checked against the American Presidency Project (UC Santa Barbara) or the
Ronald Reagan Presidential Library. Copy from those sources when writing; never from memory.

## Notes for the writer

- Quotes 1 and 6 use 1981 numbers. The narrator says so; the essay never presents them as today's.
- Quote 8 is the hand-off: Gary hears "We the People" through the screen door, and the series
  is about people forming teams. Mike pauses Spotify right after it.
- The episode's closing line ("So what's my time for choosing?") calls back to the 1964 speech
  named in the introduction.
- Audio: the narrator reads the quotes. If James wants Reagan's own voice, use the Reagan
  Library's original recordings of these presidential speeches, not a repackaged podcast that
  claims its own copyright.

## Held for later episodes (verified, not used here)
