# Civic Encounters: Teams — opening quotes (DRAFT)

Status: Draft. Quotes are supplied by James; AI does not choose them.
Rule (James, Nov 16, 2026): every essay opens with one major quote, then the story begins.

## How each opening works
1. The quote, read by the narrator, with the speaker's name.
2. One short line of context if needed (who said it and when).
3. The story starts.

## Checks before an essay is written
- Exact wording copied from a primary source (speech transcript, book, official record).
- Speaker, date and source recorded below.
- Rights: public-domain or government text can run long; anything else stays short.
- Episode 1 note: the series rule says no economist quote in episode 1's lead-in. If James's
  episode 1 quote is from an economist, James decides whether that rule changes.
