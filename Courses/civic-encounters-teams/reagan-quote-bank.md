## Rules
1. A quote is used word for word from a primary source, or not at all.
2. One quote, one main use in the series. A second use (for example, a callback) must be logged
   and marked "Callback."
3. Standard and Short versions log separately.
4. Status moves: Planned → In beat sheet → In essay (draft) → Approved by James → Narrated.
