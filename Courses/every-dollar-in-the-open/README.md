# Every Dollar in the Open

**Status:** Draft. Six lessons; series complete. Nothing here is approved.

This series is not about cleaning up the current system. It lays a new foundation: an agentic
system built on the Founders and Framers of the Republic, starting with a team's own money. It
opens with a plain explainer, then follows one Sedgwick County team from a cash envelope to a registered group testing the
plan. Planned for teams starting **2027-01-01** (Master-Plan T20, T20a, T20b).

**The Founders' stones, one per lesson:** open accounts, Article I §9 (01); few and defined
powers, Federalist No. 45 (02); money follows consent, Virginia Statute for Religious Freedom
(03); Hamilton's sinking fund, Report on Public Credit (04); property, not promises (05); gold
and silver as the measure, Article I §10 (06). Each member's AI assistant checks the record;
people decide.

**The method:** a bank account; monthly deposit and spending files in GitHub; each file's
fingerprint stamped on the Bitcoin blockchain with OpenTimestamps; private receipt codes
(only their hash is public); $10 blocks drawn at random, seeded by a Bitcoin block hash;
change blocks when a spend splits a block; SPEND issues approved by the second admin;
an enumerated spending list; pledge blocks for optional functions.

**AGCL points the series teaches (no new doctrine):** Point 05.1 Power of 72 Sinking Liberty
Fund (lesson 04); Point 04 Social Security Gold Lockbox and Point 10 gold-backed Medicare
(lessons 05–06); draft control 00L citizen trigger (lesson 04).

**Findings and open questions raised in lesson 06:** Point 04 requires month-by-month
contribution data, but the public Social Security earnings record shows yearly earnings only;
the source of the gold and its custody; the transition for people already drawing benefits;
people who paid in before becoming citizens.
